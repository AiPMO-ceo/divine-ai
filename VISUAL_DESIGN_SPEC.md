# Blueprint Guardians — Visual Design Specification
**Ai PMO · Divine AI · aipmo.store/blueprint-lab**
Version 1.0 · May 2026

---

## The One Rule That Governs Everything

The world is calm. The characters carry the color.

Every visual decision in Blueprint Guardians flows from this single principle. The environment is neutral, warm, and restrained. Phoenix and Dragon bring controlled pops of color. The Unguided Collective drains warmth from every scene they enter. Color is never decorative. Color always means something.

---

## 1. Art Direction

### Style
Luxury soft isometric. Clean geometric environments with rounded corners. Soft ambient lighting from the top-left. No harsh edges anywhere. The game world feels like a premium architectural model brought to life.

### Feeling
Elevated. Calm. Intentional. Like the living room in the reference image: creamy off-white walls, sage mint accents, warm neutral surfaces, gold hardware details, generous white space, soft ambient lighting. Every business interior in Lumina City should feel like a room in that space.

### What This Is NOT
- Not cartoonish
- Not dark or gritty
- Not neon or glowing
- Not generic RPG pixel art
- Not corporate flat design

## 2. Color Palette

### The Core Colors

| Name | Hex | Role | Used For |
|------|-----|------|----------|
| Cream | #F8F5F0 | World base | Walls, ceilings, UI backgrounds |
| Warm Gray | #E8E5E0 | Surfaces | Floors, counters, furniture |
| Sage Mint | #A8DED0 | Environment accent | Accent panels, plants, platform surfaces |
| Bright Mint | #3DD6A3 | Governance signal | Correct answers, locked pillars, Phoenix abilities |
| Polished Navy | #0A1628 | Accent | Text, HUD elements, Dragon wings |
| Warm Gold | #C9A84C | Detail | Dragon circuit lines, trim, Blueprint Points |
| Copper Rose | #E8A090 | Phoenix color | Phoenix wing tips only |
| Cool Gray | #8AA8B8 | Villain | All Unguided Collective characters |
| Neutral | #D0CBC4 | Inactive UI | Inactive pillar dots, disabled states |
| Warm Muted | #6B6860 | Secondary text | Labels, metadata, hints |

### The Color Restraint System

**Rule 1: The world is neutral. Always.**
Floors, walls, and surfaces use cream, warm gray, and sage mint only. No other color in the environment. The world is the canvas.

**Rule 2: Characters carry the color. Sparingly.**
Dragon's gold circuit lines. Phoenix's copper rose and teal feather gradients. Color lives at the edges and details, not the body mass. Test: if you can remove the color and still read the character, reduce it.

**Rule 3: Bright mint is the governance signal.**
Bright mint (#3DD6A3) appears only when something correct, safe, or governed is happening. Correct answers, locked pillars, Phoenix abilities, successful governance actions. Never used decoratively.

**Rule 4: Villains are cold and colorless.**
The Unguided Collective uses cool gray, charcoal, and silver only. No warmth anywhere on them. No mint. No gold. No copper. Their coldness against the warm cream world is the visual warning signal.

**Rule 5: The test.**
Before adding any color, ask: if I remove this color, does the scene lose meaning? If yes, the color earns its place. If no, remove it.

## 3. Environment Design

### Lumina City — The World

Soft 30-degree isometric perspective. Every building has clean geometric lines with rounded corners. The city feels like a premium architectural model from above.

Street level: warm neutral gray (#E8E5E0), smooth like polished concrete.
Rooftops: sage mint (#A8DED0) with soft shadow depth underneath.
Building facades: creamy off-white (#F8F5F0) with minimal detail.
Gold trim lines at architectural edges: maximum 1px, 30% opacity.

### Business Interiors

Each business is a soft isometric diorama. Think a luxury dollhouse with perfect proportions and intentional negative space. Every object placed with intention. Nothing cluttered.

**Lumina Med Spa (Level 1)**
- Walls: cream (#F8F5F0)
- Floor: warm gray (#E8E5E0), subtle texture
- Accent panels: sage mint (#A8DED0)
- Reception desk: warm gray with gold trim detail (#C9A84C at 30%)
- Plants: sage mint ceramic pots
- Lighting: warm ambient from above

**Lumina Legal Office (Level 2)**
- Walls: deep cream (#F4F1EC)
- Floor: warm gray carpet texture
- Bookshelf wall: navy (#0A1628) with cream book spines
- Desk: warm gray with gold lamp detail
- Feeling: confident, expensive, calm

**Lumina HVAC Company (Level 3)**
- Walls: cream, slightly warmer tone
- Floor: warm gray, industrial but clean
- Equipment panels: navy (#0A1628) with sage mint indicator lights
- Feeling: professional, clean, not sterile

**Lumina Solo Studio (Level 4)**
- Walls: cream with one sage mint accent wall
- Floor: warm gray
- Desk accessories: gold details
- Plants throughout
- Feeling: creative but elevated

**Lumina Hiring Platform (Level 5)**
- Walls: cream and neutral gray
- Furniture: navy accent pieces
- Plants: sage mint
- Feeling: corporate but soft

**The Void Tower (Final Boss)**
- Everything inverts here. Cold. Dark navy walls. Cool gray floors.
- The only level that breaks the warm world palette intentionally.
- As the player defeats each phase, warmth slowly returns to the environment.

## 4. Character Design

### Phoenix — The Shield

**Body:** Primarily sage mint (#A8DED0). Clean, smooth, rounded form.
**Wings:** Gradient from sage mint at the base to copper rose (#E8A090) at the tips. Color blooms outward, never at the center.
**Eyes:** Bright mint (#3DD6A3) with a soft warm inner glow.
**Abilities:**
- Illuminate: soft radial bloom of bright mint light spreading from her position. Hidden gaps appear with a gentle gold pulse outline.
- Dome of Standards: wings fold forward into a luminous cream-mint dome.
- Restoration: warm gold-mint light washes over the affected area.
- Blueprint Pulse: full-screen bloom of bright mint, 2-second duration, then fades.

**The rule for Phoenix:** Her form is sage mint and cream. Copper rose and bright mint appear only at the tips of wings and in ability activations. If you see bright mint or copper rose on her body mass, reduce it.

### Dragon — The Sword

**Body:** Dark gray-green (#4A5568 base). Sleek, precise, angular form.
**Scales:** Cool gray (#8AA8B8) highlights along the spine and shoulders.
**Circuit lines:** Warm gold (#C9A84C) tracing the joints and spine. This is Dragon's signature detail. Gold lives only in these lines, nowhere else.
**Wings:** Navy (#0A1628), dark, wide, deliberate.
**Eyes:** Gold (#C9A84C) with a focused, targeting quality.
**Abilities:**
- Boundary Strike: clean gold line left behind at strike point. Soft permanent glow.
- Precision Cut: gold targeting beam from eyes to violation point.
- The Standard: navy governance flag planted, gold seal at center.
- Blueprint Blade: full gold charge radiating from Dragon's body for 1 second, then precise strike.

**The rule for Dragon:** His form is dark gray-green and navy. Gold lives only in the circuit lines and ability effects. No warmth in his body mass. He looks expensive, precise, and cold until he acts.

### The Unguided Collective

**All commanders share the same palette restraint:**
- Cool gray (#8AA8B8) as primary body color
- Charcoal (#3A3A3A) for clothing and accessories
- Silver (#C0C8D0) for metallic details
- No mint. No gold. No copper. No warmth.

**NULL (the leader):**
- Entirely dark charcoal suit
- Face replaced by a smooth dark screen displaying a shifting AI-generated persona
- The persona shifts between warm-looking faces to seem trustworthy
- Phoenix Illuminate reveals his true form: a blank dark screen

**The environmental effect of villains:**
As a villain commander approaches, the ambient light temperature in that zone cools by approximately 10 degrees toward blue-gray. Imperceptible at maximum distance. Clearly visible at pitch distance. This is not a color change on the environment objects. It is a subtle overlay tint on the entire zone.

## 5. UI Design System

### Philosophy
Neumorphic cream panels. Soft inset shadows. White space used generously. Every UI element should feel like it belongs in the same luxury cream world as the environment, not like it was dropped in from a different application.

### Panel Style
- Background: cream (#F8F5F0) or white (#FFFFFF)
- Border: 0.5px solid #E0DDD8
- Border radius: 14px for panels, 10px for cards, 99px for pills and badges
- No harsh drop shadows. Soft inset shadow at 8% navy opacity maximum.

### HUD Elements

**Blueprint Points Counter:**
- Cream pill badge (#FFFFFF background)
- Navy label text (#0A1628)
- Gold value number (#C9A84C)
- Border: 0.5px solid #E0DDD8

**Certification Tier Badge:**
- Navy background (#0A1628)
- Sage mint text (#A8DED0)
- Pill shape, 99px border radius

**Pillar Tracker (7 dots):**
- Inactive: neutral gray (#D0CBC4) with 0.5px border
- Locked: bright mint (#3DD6A3), soft glow
- Active (current): gold (#C9A84C), pulsing gently

**Illuminate Uses:**
- Active: bright mint (#3DD6A3) circles
- Used: neutral gray (#D0CBC4) circles
- Never show a number alone. Always pair with the circle icons.

### Challenge Panel

Background: cream (#F8F5F0) at 95% opacity over game world.
Border radius: 16px.
Border: 0.5px solid #E0DDD8.

**Answer choice states:**
- Default: white (#FFFFFF) background, navy text, 0.5px neutral border
- Hover: cream (#F8F5F0) background, slight border darkening
- Correct: light mint background (#E8F7F3), mint border (#3DD6A3), mint dark label (#0F6E56)
- Villain trap: light rose background (#FEF0ED), warm border (#D4917A), red label (#A32D2D)
- Wrong: light gray background (#F0EDE8), muted border, gray label

**Villain trap visual treatment:**
The villain trap answer choice has a subtle warm rose tint. Not red. Not alarming. Warm enough to feel inviting, which is the point. The trap should look almost as appealing as the correct answer. If it looks obviously wrong, it has failed its design purpose.

### Dialogue Panel

Speaker portrait: circular, 48px, cream border ring.
Speaker name: in their signature color (Phoenix in mint, Dragon in gold, villains in cool gray).
Dialogue text: Manrope 13px, navy (#0A1628), line height 1.6.
Text animates character by character at 30 characters per second.
Background: cream panel, same style as challenge panel.

## 6. Typography

### Font Pairing
- Display: Instrument Serif — used for level titles, chapter headings, CTA text. Warm, confident, editorial.
- UI and body: Manrope — used for all UI labels, dialogue text, HUD, challenge questions. Clean, modern, highly legible at small sizes.

### Type Scale

| Use | Font | Size | Weight | Color |
|-----|------|------|--------|-------|
| Level title | Instrument Serif | 20px | Regular | Navy #0A1628 |
| Section heading | Manrope | 16px | 500 | Navy #0A1628 |
| UI label | Manrope | 11px | 500 uppercase | Warm muted #6B6860 |
| Challenge question | Manrope | 13px | 500 | Navy #0A1628 |
| Answer choice | Manrope | 12px | 400 | Navy #0A1628 |
| Dialogue text | Manrope | 13px | 400 | Navy #0A1628 |
| HUD points | Manrope | 14px | 500 | Gold #C9A84C |
| Hint text | Manrope | 11px | 400 italic | Warm muted #6B6860 |
| Governance principle | Manrope | 12px | 400 italic | Mint dark #0F6E56 |

### Typography Rules
- Sentence case always. Never title case. Never all caps except for the shortest UI tags (3 words maximum).
- No text below 11px in any UI element.
- Line height 1.6 for all body and dialogue text.
- Letter spacing 0.07em for uppercase labels only.

## 7. Lighting and Shadow

### Ambient Lighting
- Source: top-left, soft warm white
- Never harsh. Never bleached. Every surface feels gently lit.
- Intensity: think afternoon light through a sheer curtain, not direct sunlight.

### Shadow Rules
- Maximum 15% opacity on all shadows.
- Circular soft shadow under every character.
- No hard drop shadows anywhere in the game world.
- Inset shadows on UI panels: 8% navy opacity, 2px blur maximum.

### Special Lighting Events

**Phoenix Illuminate ability:**
Soft radial bloom of bright mint (#3DD6A3) spreading from her position over 0.5 seconds. Hidden governance gaps appear with a gentle gold pulse outline (#C9A84C at 40% opacity). Bloom fades over 1 second. Never harsh. Never neon.

**Villain proximity effect:**
As a villain enters within 200px of an NPC, the ambient light in that zone cools by approximately 10 degrees toward blue-gray. Not a sudden change. A gradual, almost imperceptible shift. The player should feel something is wrong before they can name what it is.

**Victory lighting:**
When a level is cleared, a warm gold wash passes over the entire environment from left to right over 1 second. Like warm late-afternoon light returning to a space. Then settles back to normal ambient.

**The Void Tower:**
The only environment that inverts the lighting system. Cool top-down light. Shadows in warm tones (the inverse of the normal world). As the player defeats each boss phase, the lighting gradually warms and the environment transitions back toward the normal world palette.

## 8. Animation Principles

### Character Movement
- Phoenix moves fluidly, slightly weightless. Her walk has a gentle float to it.
- Dragon moves with precision and weight. Each step deliberate.
- NPCs move in gentle idle animations: subtle breathing, small gestures. Never static.
- Villains move with smooth corporate efficiency. Nothing wasted. Nothing warm.

### UI Animations
- Panel appearance: fade in over 0.2 seconds. No slide. No bounce.
- Correct answer: gentle mint bloom pulse on the card, 0.3 seconds.
- Wrong answer: soft warm rose pulse on the card, 0.3 seconds. No shake. No harsh flash.
- Pillar lock: dot fills with mint, soft glow pulse 3 times, then holds.
- Blueprint Points: counter increments smoothly over 0.5 seconds. Never jumps.
- Tier unlock: gold wash across the tier badge, soft mint text fade in.

### The No-Harsh-Flash Rule
No sudden bright flashes anywhere in the game. This is both an aesthetic choice and an accessibility requirement for photosensitive players. Every light event is a bloom, a fade, or a wash. Never a flash.
