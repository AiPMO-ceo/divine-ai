# Divine AI by Ai PMO

**Open source AI governance tools for small businesses, neurodivergent founders, and underserved communities.**

> We do not make decisions for your business. We illuminate what was previously unseen.

---

## What is Divine AI?

Divine AI is an open source governance tool suite designed to help small businesses identify where their AI use carries risk, train their teams through real-world scenarios, and take informed next steps.

Most small businesses are deploying AI without guardrails, without policies, and without any idea where their exposure lives. They are not being careless. They are being underserved.

Divine AI was built to change that.

## The Blueprint Lab

All tools live at **[aipmo.store/blueprint-lab](https://aipmo.store/blueprint-lab)**

### Tool 1: The Divine Digital Blueprint Assessment
An interactive scored assessment across 7 governance pillars. Users answer plain-language questions about how they use AI today, receive a score on a 28-point scale, are assigned a certification tier, and leave with a personalized action plan built around their specific gaps.

### Tool 2: The Cost of Not Knowing
A branching scenario-based training story that places employees inside a real-world situation, lets them make decisions, and shows the consequences of guided versus unguided AI use. Built specifically for visual learners, neurodivergent thinkers, autistic individuals, and ADHD minds.

## The Seven Pillars

Divine AI is built on the Divine Digital Blueprint, a governance framework authored by Yancy Glass and aligned with UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of AI and the OECD AI Principles.

| Pillar | Question it answers |
|--------|-------------------|
| Purpose Architecture | Does your AI have a defined reason for existing? |
| Values Alignment | Does your AI reflect your brand ethics, not just complete tasks? |
| Safeguard Infrastructure | Do you have documented limits on what your AI can say and do? |
| Stakeholder Sovereignty | Can your customers and team reach a human when AI falls short? |
| Inclusive Access | Does your AI work for every customer you serve? |
| Governance and Accountability | Is someone assigned to own AI performance? |
| Continuous Wisdom | Do you have a process for growing your governance as your business grows? |

## Who This Was Built For

Divine AI was designed with the following communities at the center:

- Small business owners deploying AI for the first time
- Autistic, neurodivergent, and ADHD individuals who learn through experience and visual storytelling
- Solo founders and underserved entrepreneurs who have been last in line for enterprise-grade guidance
- Frontline employees using AI daily without training or policies
- Any business that believes governance should not require a legal budget

## Open Source Philosophy

The Divine Digital Blueprint is a living document.

We authored the foundation. The small business owners, the neurodivergent founders, the underserved entrepreneurs, they are the co-authors of everything that comes next.

This framework is designed to grow with the communities that use it. Fork it. Adapt it. Build on it. Add what your community needs. That is the point.

We are only the beginning.
