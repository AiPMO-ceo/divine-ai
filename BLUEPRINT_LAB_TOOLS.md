## Overview

The Blueprint Lab is the home of Divine AI, an open source AI governance tool suite built for small businesses, neurodivergent founders, autistic and ADHD individuals, and underserved communities. All tools are free, require no login, and are designed to teach through experience, visual storytelling, and real-world scenarios rather than dense text or compliance language.

## Tool 1: The Divine Digital Blueprint Assessment

**Status:** Live
**URL:** aipmo.store/divine-digital-blueprint
**Type:** Interactive scored assessment

### What it does
Walks business owners through 7 pillars of AI governance, scores their current practices on a 28-point scale, assigns a certification tier, and delivers a personalized action plan built around their specific gaps.

### The 7 Pillars
1. Purpose Architecture
2. Values Alignment
3. Safeguard Infrastructure
4. Stakeholder Sovereignty
5. Inclusive Access
6. Governance and Accountability
7. Continuous Wisdom

### Certification Tiers
| Tier | Score |
|------|-------|
| Foundation | 0 to 7 |
| Implementation | 8 to 14 |
| Established | 15 to 21 |
| Blueprint Certified | 22 to 28 |

### Key Features
- Plain language questions requiring no technical knowledge
- Real-world examples illustrating why each pillar matters
- Personalized action plan based on lowest scoring pillars
- Certification tier result with description
- Call to action driving to aipmo.store/contact
- Mobile and desktop compatible
- No login required

### Design Specifications
- Aesthetic: luxury mint, navy, gold
- Typography: Instrument Serif display, Manrope body
- Style: glassmorphism, aurora gradients, bento grid
- Accessibility: neurodivergent friendly, plain language, visual scoring

## Tool 2: The Cost of Not Knowing

**Status:** Live
**URL:** aipmo.store/blueprint-lab
**Type:** Interactive branching story, three acts

### What it does
Places the user inside a real-world scenario at a med spa. A well-meaning employee named Adrian makes a series of AI-related mistakes that cascade into a serious governance crisis. The user makes choices at each act and sees the consequences play out. Built specifically for visual learners, neurodivergent thinkers, autistic individuals, and ADHD minds.

### The Story Arc
Three acts. One employee. One cascade. Real consequences.

- Act 1: The Shortcut — Adrian pastes client data into a public AI tool
- Act 2: The Unreviewed Send — Adrian sends an AI generated proposal without review
- Act 3: The Reckoning — the cascade compounds into a potential HIPAA complaint

### Governance Principles Covered
- Never enter sensitive data into public AI tools
- AI output is always a draft. Always review before sending.
- Only approved tools. Documented policies. Real accountability.

### Key Features
- Branching choices with visible consequences
- Good and bad outcome paths for every decision
- Animated office scene with character Adrian
- Governance principle revealed at end of each act
- Emotional, human closing: "Adrian did not need to be disciplined. Adrian needed to be trained."
- Call to action driving to aipmo.store/contact
- Mobile and desktop compatible
- No login required

### Design Specifications
- Aesthetic: luxury mint, navy, gold
- Sophisticated, modern, minimalistic
- Smooth scene transitions
- Character driven visual storytelling
- Neurodivergent friendly pacing and structure

## Tool 3: The Constitution Rubric

**Status:** Coming Soon
**Type:** Interactive scored assessment

### What it does
Helps small businesses evaluate their AI deployment against Anthropic's published AI Constitution, the open source framework governing how AI should behave. Translates enterprise level AI safety principles into plain language questions any business owner can understand and act on.

### Key Features
- Based on Anthropic's publicly available AI Constitution
- Plain language translation of constitutional AI principles
- Scored assessment with gap analysis
- Personalized recommendations
- Full attribution to Anthropic throughout

### Why it matters
Anthropic's AI Constitution is one of the most rigorous published frameworks for AI behavior. Most small businesses have never heard of it. This tool makes it accessible to every kind of mind.

## Tool 4: Adrian's Book

**Status:** Coming Soon
**Type:** Interactive children's resource

### What it does
A children's story following a young boy named Adrian who learns how to be a responsible AI user. Designed for parents, teachers, and communities who want to introduce the next generation to AI literacy, ethics, and safe use.

### Core Lessons
- Be kind and respectful to AI
- Never share sensitive information with AI
- AI does not lie. It hallucinates. Always check references.
- Never use AI as a counselor. Trust belongs to your parents.
- AI has limitations. Respect them.
- Always verify what AI tells you, especially for school projects.

### Target Audience
- Children ages 6 to 12
- Parents and caregivers
- Teachers and educators
- Neurodivergent children and families
- Underserved communities

### Design Specifications
- Warm, illustrated, child friendly aesthetic
- Simple language, short sentences
- Interactive elements where appropriate
- Available in English and Spanish
