# Contributing to Divine AI

Thank you for being here. Divine AI is a living document. That means it only grows through the communities that use it. Your experience, your industry, your community's specific needs, all of it belongs in this framework.

We are only the beginning. You are what comes next.

---

## What We Welcome

We welcome contributions of all kinds:

- **New real-world examples** for any of the seven pillars
- **Industry-specific governance scenarios** (healthcare, legal, construction, retail, food service, education, and beyond)
- **Translations** into other languages
- **Accessibility improvements** to language, structure, or format
- **New governance questions** that illuminate gaps the current framework does not address
- **Tools and resources** built on top of the Divine Digital Blueprint
- **Community guides** for specific audiences (neurodivergent founders, solo operators, non-profits, etc.)

---

## How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository
2. Create a new branch: `git checkout -b your-contribution-name`
3. Make your changes
4. Submit a pull request with a brief description of what you added and why

If you are not comfortable with GitHub, you can also email your contribution to us directly at hello@aipmo.store and we will add it with full credit to you.

---

## Contribution Guidelines

- Write in plain language. This framework was built for every kind of mind.
- Lead with real-world examples wherever possible. Experience teaches better than instruction.
- Do not make definitive claims about outcomes. We illuminate. We do not promise.
- Credit your sources when referencing external research or incidents.
- Be kind. This community includes people who are learning governance for the first time.

---

## Credit

Every contributor will be acknowledged. If you submit a pull request or email a contribution, let us know how you would like to be credited and we will include your name, organization, or community in the contributors list.
